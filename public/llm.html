What B Design does

B Design is a one-click AI creative generation platform. A user uploads a reference layout, picks a brand from their Brand Hub, chooses an aspect ratio, and receives a finished on-brand ad creative in seconds — with the brand's logo, colours, typography rules, and voice respected automatically.

It is purpose-built for high-volume creative production: real estate launches, education campaigns, fashion drops, festive ads, and performance marketing variants.

Who it is for

  • Marketing agencies producing creatives for many brands at once.
  • In-house brand and performance teams who need on-brand variants daily.
  • Founders and marketers who want professional ad creatives without a designer in the loop for every iteration.

What is offered

  • Brand Hub — store logos, colour palettes, typography, voice, brand brief, example copy, and never-rules per brand.
  • Studio — one-click generation from a reference image plus a selected brand.
  • Multiple formats — square (1:1), portrait (4:5), landscape (16:9), and story (9:16).
  • History — every generation is saved alongside its reference for review and iteration.
  • Chrome extension — right-click any image on the web to send it into B Design as a reference.
  • Admin tools — multi-account control, credit allocation, and global usage analytics for agency owners.

How it is different

  • Reference images are used as layout blueprints only — never as a source of subjects, names, locations, or pricing.
  • Brand assets (logos, 3D architectural renders) are preserved with strict fidelity.
  • Strict separation between text and imagery — headlines never sit on top of focal subjects.
  • Copy is generated fresh each time, with rotating creative moods, instead of recycling brief examples verbatim.
  • Built by an agency (Because) for agency workflows — not a generic AI tool retrofitted for marketing.
